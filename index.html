Mission 01 · Halal & Thayyib

Discovery Learning: observe → question → gather evidence → conclude.
📖

Learning Foundation

Surah Al-Baqarah (2:168) instructs people to consume what is lawful and good (thayyib) and to avoid the ways of Satan.
☪️

Halal

Something permitted under Islamic law. Animal-derived ingredients require verification of their origin and processing, including slaughter.
🌿

Thayyib

Good, clean, wholesome, and safe to consume. Halal food is not necessarily thayyib if it is contaminated, spoiled, or expired.

Mission 02 · Critical Ingredient Lab

Review critical points and verification steps for each ingredient.

🧬 Gelatin

A collagen-derived ingredient extracted from animal skin or bones. Porcine gelatin is haram; bovine gelatin requires confirmation of its source and slaughter method.

Check the source

🧈 E471

Mono- and diglycerides may come from vegetable oils or animal fats. The code alone does not establish the source.

Critical point

🔴 E120

Carmine/cochineal is a red colorant derived from insects. The module notes differing views; follow the guidance of the relevant authority or school of thought.

Scholarly views differ

🥛 Whey powder

A by-product of cheese production. Check the enzyme/rennet used and verify its source.

Check processing

🐖 Lard / pork fat

Pig fat. This ingredient must be avoided.

Haram

🧾 Labels and certificates

Check the ingredient list, manufacturer, certificate number and validity, and issuing body. Do not determine a product’s status from packaging appearance alone.

Verify the evidence
An E-number is not automatically haram. Check what the code refers to and the ingredient’s source. If information is insufficient, mark it as unverified rather than treating an assumption as fact.

Preparing Assemblr Cards

  1. Create or select a learning project in Assemblr and arrange 3D content for the five cases.
  2. Copy the project or experience link that students can access.
  3. Generate a QR code containing that link and print it on a case card.
  4. Scan the QR code in this app. It will offer to open the Assemblr link.

QR scanning only reads and opens a link. Image tracking and 3D display take place in Assemblr according to the project’s features and configuration; this HTML does not create them automatically.
